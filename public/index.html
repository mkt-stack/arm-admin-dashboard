    Affiliate revenue & commission (by order date)

    Video GMV: GQ vs affiliate

    Monthly trend (revenue & new revenue videos)

    How these numbers are counted
    • Periods are Bangkok calendar days. “vs prev” compares with the previous period of the same length.
    • Revenue, orders, items, commission, returns: affiliate order lines by order date, excluding cancelled/unpaid. Revenue = unit price × quantity. Commission = creator + shop-ads commission (actual once settled, otherwise estimated).
    • Views, likes, impressions, clicks, video GMV: TikTok’s daily video metrics. CTR = product clicks ÷ product impressions. Conversion = video SKU orders ÷ product clicks.
    • New revenue videos: posted in the period and with video GMV > 0 in the period.
    • GQ = the shop’s own official and marketing accounts; everything else is affiliate.
    • ARM match: TikTok handle on the ARM profile (case-insensitive, “@” and link prefixes ignored).
    • Product-card vs organic split is not available from TikTok. Sample cost & ROI arrive with the Shopify sample data.
    • Data is copied daily from IT’s TikTok sync (ready after ~14:00 Bangkok for the previous day).

    Customer notification

    Endpoint fired when a profile is successfully created/updated in Shopify — replaces a customer-facing "you're all set" message. Also fired on a successful manual sync-task retry.

    Send automatically on

    Turning an event off stops the automatic notification for it (sync-task retries included). The manual Notify button on a profile always sends.
